01 / COAST

River No. 2 Beach

A well-known beach destination outside Freetown, appreciated for its scenic coastline and relaxing atmosphere. Visitors should respect local guidance and keep the beach clean.

Good for: Beach visits, photography and relaxation.

02 / WILDLIFE

Tiwai Island

A nature-focused destination associated with rainforest habitat and wildlife. Plan visits with appropriate local guides and follow conservation rules.

Good for: Nature learning, wildlife and eco-tourism.

03 / ISLANDS

Banana Islands

An island group off the Freetown Peninsula with coastal scenery and cultural history. Check transport arrangements and local conditions before travelling.

Good for: Island exploration, heritage and scenic views.

04 / CITY

Freetown

Sierra Leone's capital is a starting point for learning about local history, markets, food, music and everyday city life.

Good for: Culture, food, history and city tours.

Travel responsibly: Confirm current transport, opening arrangements, safety guidance and prices with trusted local sources before departure.
