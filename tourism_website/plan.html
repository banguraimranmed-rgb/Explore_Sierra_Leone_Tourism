BEFORE YOU GO

Your travel checklist

Tick each item as you prepare. This checklist works in your browser while the page remains open.

Responsible travel tips

  • Ask permission before photographing people.
  • Use local guides and businesses where possible.
  • Avoid littering and disturbing wildlife.
  • Keep valuables secure and follow current local advice.
  • Verify prices and transport details before setting off.
